# What Loremetry Does

Loremetry is a desktop app for writers. You bring in your manuscript folders, keep your project organized on your own computer, and run craft and publishing reports against your work. Some reports run entirely offline. Others use AI when you want deeper feedback.

It is not a generic grammar checker. It is built around how novels and books are actually written and published: chapters, scenes, characters, continuity, pacing, blurbs, store metadata, and print prep.

## In one sentence

Loremetry helps you revise and prepare a manuscript by organizing the project on disk and producing author-facing reports about craft, continuity, engagement, and publishing.

## Who it is for

Authors of fiction and non-fiction who keep manuscripts as files (especially markdown chapter/scene trees) and want structured feedback without giving up control of their drafts.

## What you do in the app

1. **Open or create a book project** on your machine. Loremetry can use a standard project layout with manuscript chapters, story elements (characters, places, themes), plot notes, and admin files.
2. **Browse and edit** that project in the app. Your writing stays as ordinary files on disk; Loremetry keeps its own local database for app data and saved reports.
3. **Choose an analysis** that matches where you are in the process — early draft craft, continuity pass, series check, or publish-ready packaging.
4. **Read the report** it produces. Reports are meant to be actionable: what to fix, where it shows up, and what to do next.
5. **Keep reports with the project** so you can revisit them as the draft changes.

## Privacy and where work runs

Loremetry is local-first. Your manuscripts live on your computer in a local database and on disk. You do not have to upload your book to use the app.

- **Local analyses** (no AI) run on your machine and can work offline. Examples include line polish, print production numbers, Vellum/Atticus prep, Zeigarnik open-loop scans, and many prose metrics.
- **AI analyses** use language models for deeper craft and market-facing reports. Some of that can run with bundled local AI; cloud AI is available when you use credits for those features.

Local reports are free. You pay only when you choose AI-powered analyses.

## Kinds of reports

Reports fall into groups. You pick the ones that match the job at hand.

### Craft — prose and structure

Looks at how the writing reads and how the story is built: show-don’t-tell, AI-sounding phrasing, story beats, scene vs reflection balance, and POV discipline (head-hopping and information leaks).

### Craft — plot and continuity

Finds contradicted facts, unpaid setups (Chekhov’s gun), dropped threads vs intentional red herrings, foreshadowing fairness, MacGuffin clarity, and confusing timeline or flashback shifts.

### Craft — character and theme

Checks external want vs internal need, whether the theme holds across scenes, and how mirror/foil characters support the theme.

### Craft — reader engagement

Scores open loops and cliffhangers, dramatic irony, and whether stakes rise or stall.

### Craft — series

Compares pacing across books, tracks setups that should pay off later, and checks motifs and themes for cohesion across a series.

### Craft — metrics

Numbers-only passes: readability, sentence and paragraph length, chapter balance, dialogue ratio, dialogue tags, passive voice, sticky sentences, repeated phrases, and opening/closing strength.

### Publish

Helps get the book ready for readers and stores: blurb variants, print page-count and spine estimates, AI beta-reader style reactions, cliffhanger and hook strength, pacing curves, line polish, and clean markdown for tools like Vellum or Atticus.

### KDP / Wide (store listing)

Helps with discovery and metadata for Amazon KDP and wider stores: genre fit, categories, keywords, competition and review insights, BISAC codes, content advisories, and copy-ready metadata sheets.

## What you get out of it

- A single place to keep a book’s files and analysis history.
- Reports written for authors, not for engineers — cite chapters, flag patterns, suggest what to change.
- Offline help for polish and production when you do not want (or need) the network.
- Optional AI depth when you want craft or market feedback that simple word counts cannot give.

## What it is not

- Not a substitute for human editors or beta readers (though some reports aim to catch issues earlier).
- Not a cloud writing platform that owns your files.
- Not only spellcheck or style tips — it covers story craft and publishing packaging as well.

## Bottom line

If you write in files and want private, structured feedback on the draft and how you will sell or format it, Loremetry is the desktop craft companion that organizes the project and runs those reports for you.
